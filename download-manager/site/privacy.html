Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026. Draft for the first public release.

The short version

Harbor runs on your PC. The URLs you download, your files and your browser cookies never leave it. We don't have accounts, and we don't sell or share data.

The app

  • The download list, settings and partial files are stored locally in Harbor's data folder.
  • The engine listens only on 127.0.0.1. Other computers can't reach it, and it rejects requests from web pages.
  • Downloads go directly from your PC to the site you download from.
  • Update check (planned): the app will fetch a small latest.json file to tell you about new versions. That request includes the app version only.
  • Usage statistics (planned, off by default): if you opt in, Harbor would send event names (such as "download finished"), the app version and a random install ID. It never sends URLs, file names or cookies. Settings will show exactly what is sent.

The browser extension

  • It watches network responses in your browser to spot media files and streams, and keeps that list in browser memory for the open tab only.
  • When you click Download, it sends the media address and that site's cookies, referer and user agent to the Harbor app (or AB Download Manager) on your own PC, so sites that require a login still work. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • Its settings (destination app, port, options) are stored in your browser's extension storage.

Purchases (once Pro is available)

Payments will be handled by a merchant of record, who will process your email and payment details under their own privacy policy. Licence keys are checked offline in the app.
