Restarts after a crash

Progress is flushed to disk every second. We kill the app mid-download in our tests: it comes back and continues on its own, and re-checks the bytes it resumes from.

SHA-256

On every file

Every finished download gets SHA-256 and MD5. Paste the hash a site publishes and Fetchline tells you if the file doesn't match.

Video button in Chrome and Edge

Hover a video and click Download. You get every quality a stream offers (1080p, 720p…) or the plain file, named after the page.

Queue, scheduler, speed limits

Run big downloads overnight, cap speed so calls stay smooth, and file everything into category folders automatically.

Works with AB Download Manager

Already use AB DM? The extension can send what it finds there instead.

What Fetchline won't do

It never downloads DRM-protected video (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and the like), or encrypted or live streams. The Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons versions of the extension also don't download from YouTube or Instagram. If that's what you need, read the FAQ. We'd rather tell you now than have you find out after installing.
