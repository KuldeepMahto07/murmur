# Referral programs

**Status: Not started.** Part of [15 · Growth](../README.md) in the [Harbor SaaS plan](../../README.md).

- **Mechanic:** "Give a month, get a month". A free user who refers a buyer gets Pro for 3 months, and the buyer gets 15% off.
- **Pro lifetime owners** earn store credit toward the Family pack instead.
- **Delivery:** referral links from the merchant of record's affiliate system, the same one as [affiliate-marketing](../../09-acquisition/affiliate-marketing/README.md).
- **Placement:** after the user's 10th verified download: "Harbor saved you N retries. Share it?" Shown once.
