# Customer interviews

**Status: Not started.** Part of [02 · Validation](../README.md) in the [Harbor SaaS plan](../../README.md).

## Goal
Find out whether people will switch from IDM, AB DM or the browser's built-in downloads to Harbor, and what they would pay.

## Who to talk to (15 interviews)
- 5 current IDM users. Recruit them from r/software, r/DataHoarder, tech Discords and IDM-related forums.
- 5 AB DM or FDM users.
- 5 students who download course videos.

## Script (30 minutes)
1. What did you download last week that was big or annoying? Walk me through it.
2. Which download manager do you use, and why? What made you pick it? Did you pay?
3. Tell me about the last time a download failed. What happened?
4. How often do you save videos from web pages? From which sites? (Note how many say YouTube.)
5. Would a checksum or "verified" badge matter to you?
6. If a tool did X for a one-time $Y, would you buy it? Try $9, $15 and $25.

## Output
Fill in a table with persona, current tool, top pain, share of YouTube use and willingness to pay. Decide on the price in [pricing-strategy](../../11-conversion/pricing-strategy/README.md).
