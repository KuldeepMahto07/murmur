# Landing page test

**Status: Not started.** Part of [02 · Validation](../README.md) in the [Fetchline SaaS plan](../../README.md).

## Test
A one-page site with three parts:
- A hero: "Downloads up to 2× faster, verified, crash-proof."
- A 20-second GIF of the video button and the segment map.
- One call to action: "Get the beta". Optionally a second button: "Pre-order Pro, $12 lifetime (early price)".

## Setup
- Hosting: a static site (GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages), with Plausible for privacy-friendly analytics.
- Traffic: 3 Reddit posts in relevant subreddits (following each sub's rules), 1 Hacker News "Show HN" once the beta is downloadable, and AlternativeTo.

## Success criteria
- At least 10% of visitors sign up for the beta from community traffic.
- At least 1% click pre-order.
- If signups fall below 5%, rewrite the message: test "verified" against "faster" against "no adware".
