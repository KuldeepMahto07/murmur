# Niche selection

**Status: Done.** Part of [01 · Idea](../README.md) in the [Harbor SaaS plan](../../README.md).

## Chosen niche
**A "clean" IDM alternative for people who download large legitimate files and openly served media.**

| Segment | Why it fits Harbor |
|---|---|
| Students and online-course users | Their course platforms often serve plain MP4 or HLS, which Harbor's video button handles |
| Developers and IT staff | They download ISOs, SDKs, datasets and VM images that need multi-connection speed and verified SHA-256 |
| Users on throttled or unstable links | Segmented downloading plus crash-safe resume solves the core pain |
| AB DM and IDM users | A friendly bridge: Harbor's video button can hand off to AB DM |

## Deliberately excluded
- YouTube, Netflix, Spotify and other DRM or protected platforms. This keeps us inside the store policies and out of a legal and patching treadmill.
- Torrents and P2P, at least until much later.

## Positioning line
"The fast, honest download manager: verified downloads, real resume, no adware, no cracks needed."
