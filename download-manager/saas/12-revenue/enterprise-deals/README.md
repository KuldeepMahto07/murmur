## Fetchline for Teams
- **Price:** $4/user/year, minimum 10 seats.
- **Features:**
  - MSI installer and Group Policy/Intune settings (proxy, folders, allowed sites, speed limits)
  - NTLM/Kerberos proxy auth
  - Central licence file
  - Extension force-install policy for Chrome and Edge
- **Who:** labs, universities, studios and IT teams that move large ISOs and datasets.
- **Sales:** inbound only at first, through a "Teams" page and a contact form. Quotes go out through the merchant of record's invoices.
- **Requires:** proxy auth and an MSI first. See [roadmap](../../03-planning/product-roadmap/README.md).
