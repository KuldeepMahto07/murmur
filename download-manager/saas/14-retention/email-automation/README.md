# Email automation

**Status: Not started.** Part of [14 · Retention](../README.md) in the [Harbor SaaS plan](../../README.md).

There is email only for people who gave it: waitlist, buyers, newsletter.

| Trigger | Email |
|---|---|
| Joined the waitlist | Welcome + a beta timeline |
| Purchase | Licence key, activation link, a 3-tip quick start |
| Day 7 after purchase | "Did you know?" scheduler and quota tips |
| New release | Changelog with one GIF (monthly at most) |
| Licence about to renew (yearly) | 7-day reminder with a one-click cancel |

**Tool:** the merchant of record's built-in emails plus Buttondown for the newsletter. No tracking pixels.
