# Pricing strategy

**Status: Draft.** Part of [11 · Conversion](../README.md) in the [Harbor SaaS plan](../../README.md).

## Anchor
IDM costs $24.95 lifetime or $11.95 a year for 1 PC (checked 2026-10-23). AB DM is free.

## Proposed prices
| Plan | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | See [freemium-model](../freemium-model/README.md) |
| **Pro lifetime, 1 user (up to 3 PCs)** | **$19** | Cheaper than IDM, and per user rather than per PC |
| Pro yearly | $9/year | For people who prefer low upfront cost; includes updates |
| Founders (pre-sale) | $12 lifetime | See [pre-sales](../../02-validation/pre-sales/README.md) |
| Regional pricing | About 40–60% off in India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Egypt and similar | Set through the merchant of record's purchasing-power pricing |

**Validate in [customer-interviews](../../02-validation/customer-interviews/README.md)** by testing $9, $15 and $25. Revisit after 100 sales.
