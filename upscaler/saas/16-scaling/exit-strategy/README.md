# Exit strategy

**Status: Not started (plan written).** Part of [16 · Scaling](../README.md) in the [Clarion SaaS plan](../../README.md).

**Options**
1. **Bootstrap for cash flow:** a high-margin credits business once workers cost $0.07 or less per credit.
2. **Strategic sale** to an editing-suite or creator-tool company (editors, stock-media platforms, GPU clouds) that wants upscaling plus an audience.
3. **Marketplace sale** (Acquire.com) at a typical SaaS multiple of ARR.

**Value drivers to build:** MRR growth, gross margin of at least 75%, an owned audience (email list, Discord, SEO traffic), clean model licensing (BSD/Apache only), and documented systems (see [systems](../systems/README.md)).
