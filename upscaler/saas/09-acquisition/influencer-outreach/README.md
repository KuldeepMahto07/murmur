# Influencer outreach

- **Tiers:** micro editing-tutorial creators (5k-100k), AI-video creators, and retro/VHS channels.
- **Offer:** a free Pro plan for 6 months, plus a 30% affiliate share (see [affiliate marketing](../affiliate-marketing/README.md)).
- **Brief:** show a real before/after on their own footage; no scripted claims. Disclose the sponsorship (#ad).
- **Goal:** 10 creators in quarter 1, each with a unique code to track installs and paid signups.
