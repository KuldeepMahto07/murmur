# Landing page test

**Goal:** measure demand before building the cloud. One page, two CTAs: **Download free (Windows)** and **Join the cloud waitlist**.

**Page outline (own copy)**
- Hero: "Make every clip crisp. 4K, 60 fps, on your own computer." Then an autoplaying wipe comparison built from our own sample (NASA astronaut photo, public domain).
- Three benefits: private and local, preview before you render, honest 4K/60 presets.
- Pricing teaser: "Free locally. Cloud from $0.25 per minute (waitlist)."
- FAQ: GPU requirements (NVIDIA 4 GB+; CPU works but is slow), formats (MP4/MOV/MKV/WebM in; H.264/H.265/ProRes out).

**Build:** a static page on Vercel, as its own project (separate from Murmur's). Use Plausible or Umami for cookieless analytics.

**Test:** $100-200 of TikTok/Reddit ads aimed at "video editing" interests. Two creatives: a before/after clip, and a "16K myth" explainer.

**Pass:** at least 4% visitor → waitlist conversion, or at least 10% download clicks.
