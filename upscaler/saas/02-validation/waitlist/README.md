# Waitlist

**Status: Not started (plan written).** Part of [02 · Validation](../README.md) in the [Clarion SaaS plan](../../README.md).

- **Tool:** a form posting to a serverless function, double opt-in via Resend/Postmark, and a CSV export.
- **Questions:** email, "What do you mostly enhance?" (phone clips / AI video / old family video / photos) and "Do you have an NVIDIA GPU?" The answers segment the list for pricing.
- **Referral boost:** "Move up the list: share your link". Each referral earns 10 free cloud minutes (see [referral programs](../../15-growth/referral-programs/README.md)).
- **Cadence:** a welcome email, a build update every 2 weeks with a before/after GIF, then a beta invite.
- **Goal:** 500 signups before the cloud beta, with at least 40% answering "no GPU".
