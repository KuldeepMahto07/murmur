# Pre-sales

- Offer a **Founding Cloud pack**: 300 cloud credits (1 credit = 1 output minute at 1080p30) for $19, valid 12 months. Sell it through a Stripe Payment Link with a full refund if the cloud doesn't launch within 90 days.
- **Cost check:** at the estimated L4 cost of about $0.13 per 1080p output minute from a 480p source (see [pricing strategy](../../11-conversion/pricing-strategy/README.md)), 300 credits cost at most about $39 if fully used. At a typical 30-40% usage rate, the expected cost is about $12-16. 4K costs 3 credits a minute and 60 fps doubles the cost, so heavier jobs burn credits faster rather than eroding margin.
- **Pass:** 30 founding packs sold, or 5% of the waitlist converts.
