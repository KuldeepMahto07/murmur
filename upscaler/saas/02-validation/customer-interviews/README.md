# Customer interviews

**Status: not started.** We have one real data point: a user wanted a 720x1280 gym clip at "16K 60 fps". After an explanation they accepted 4K60, and the render took 56 min on a GTX 1650 Ti.

**Target:** 15 interviews (8 short-form creators, 4 GenAI video creators, 3 people restoring family video).

**Where to find them:** r/VideoEditing, r/editors, r/aivideo, r/NewTubers, CapCut/Kling/Runway Discord servers, and local photo-restoration Facebook groups.

**Script (20 min)**
1. Tell me about the last time a video or photo looked too blurry or low-res to use. What did you do?
2. Which tools have you tried (CapCut enhancer, Topaz, Upscayl…)? What annoyed you?
3. What resolution and frame rate do you actually post at? Does 4K matter to your audience?
4. How long would you wait for a 15-second clip? A 3-minute clip?
5. Would you install a desktop app, or must it run in the browser? Do you have an NVIDIA GPU?
6. What have you paid for editing tools in the last year?
7. Show the Clarion wipe compare on their own clip, then ask: "What would you pay to do this monthly?"

**Record:** segment, tools, GPU (yes/no), max wait, price anchor, quotes. Use `interviews.csv` in this folder.

**Success:** at least 60% say they'd use it monthly, and at least 40% have no capable GPU (which justifies the cloud).
