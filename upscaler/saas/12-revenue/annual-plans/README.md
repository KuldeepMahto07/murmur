# Annual plans

- 2 months free (Creator $120/yr, Pro $290/yr) with credits granted monthly, not all up front, to limit cost exposure.
- Only offer annual Pro once cost per credit is at or below $0.07 (see the margin table in [pricing strategy](../../11-conversion/pricing-strategy/README.md)).
- Renewal reminder 14 days before, with a usage summary ("You enhanced 3.2 h of video this year").
