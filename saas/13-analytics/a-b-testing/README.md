# A/B testing

**Backlog (one test at a time, ≥ 1,000 visitors or 2 weeks each):**
1. Landing headline: privacy-led ("without uploading") vs. speed-led ("in minutes on your PC").
2. Pricing display: $49 one-time vs. "$49 once vs. $240 a year for cloud" comparison framing.
3. Trial start: after the first transcript vs. at install (in-app; needs opt-in events).
4. Pro gate copy: "Unlock Precise mode" vs. "Get the most accurate model".

**Method:** split by link or cookie-free hash on the website. In the app, a random variant is stored locally. Decide using a pre-set minimum detectable effect, and don't peek early.
