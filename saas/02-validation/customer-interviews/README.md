# Customer interviews

**Status: Not started (plan written).** Part of [02 · Validation](../README.md) in the [Murmur SaaS plan](../../README.md).

**Goal:** 15 interviews in 2 weeks (10 qualitative researchers, 3 journalists, 2 legal/HR).

## Recruit
- r/PhD, r/AskAcademia, r/GradSchool, r/qualitativeresearch: "I'm building an offline transcription tool. 20-minute chat, $15 gift card."
- University methods mailing lists and graduate-school Slack/Discord servers.
- Journalism: NICAR and Online News Association communities, local press clubs.

## Script (20 min, don't pitch until the end)
1. Tell me about the last time you had to transcribe something. What did you use?
2. What rules apply to where that audio can go? Who set them? What happens if you break them?
3. How long did it take, and what did it cost (money and hours)?
4. What did you do with the transcript afterwards (NVivo, Word, subtitles)?
5. What computer do you use? (Ask them to check: Windows or Mac? NVIDIA GPU?)
6. *Demo* the Vercel sample, then the local app if possible. What's missing? What would stop you using it?
7. Would you pay $49 once? Who would pay: you, a grant or your department?

## Record
`interviews.csv` next to this README (date, segment, constraint hard or soft, current tool, GPU yes/no, would pay, quote). Keep the transcripts local, and use Murmur to transcribe them.
