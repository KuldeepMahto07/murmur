# Beta testing

**Built:** none. No external testers yet.

**Plan:**
1. Recruit 30 testers from the waitlist (20 researchers, 5 journalists, 5 legal/HR) with mixed hardware: at least 10 without an NVIDIA GPU.
2. Ship the v0.2 installer to them plus a 5-question in-app feedback link.
3. Ask each to transcribe 3 real recordings and report: install time, first-transcript time, corrections needed per 10 minutes, and whether their export opened in their tool.
4. Exit criteria for public release: ≥ 80% install without help, a median of ≤ 5 corrections per 10 minutes on clear English audio, zero data-loss bugs.
