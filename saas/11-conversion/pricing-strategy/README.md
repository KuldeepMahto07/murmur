# Pricing strategy

**Status: Not started (plan written).** Part of [11 · Conversion](../README.md) in the [Murmur SaaS plan](../../README.md).

- **Anchor:** cloud tools cost $120–240 a year (TurboScribe $10–20/mo, Otter Pro $16.99/mo). Human transcription costs about $90/hour of audio.
- **Pro at $49 one-time** = "less than 3 months of a cloud subscription, yours forever". Paid upgrades for major versions (v2) at 50% off for existing owners.
- **Student price:** $29 with .edu or ac.* verification.
- **Team server:** $499 per server per year, up to 25 users; $999 for unlimited users.
- **Test:** $39 vs $49 vs $59 on the founding page in [02-validation/pre-sales](../../02-validation/pre-sales/) (split traffic by link, not by user).
