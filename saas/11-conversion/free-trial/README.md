# Free trial

- **Pro trial:** 7 days of full Pro, starting automatically after the **first completed transcript**, not at install. Seeing a real transcript is the "aha" moment.
- Works offline: the trial end date is stored locally and signed. Accept that some people will game it, since the cost of policing is higher than the loss.
- **Day 5 in-app note:** "Your Pro trial ends in 2 days. You've transcribed X hours." Show the value, then the price.
