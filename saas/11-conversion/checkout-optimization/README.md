# Checkout optimization

**Status: Not started (plan written).** Part of [11 · Conversion](../README.md) in the [Murmur SaaS plan](../../README.md).

- **Merchant of record:** Lemon Squeezy or Paddle (handles VAT/GST, invoices for university purchasing, PayPal, cards).
- **In-app "Upgrade" opens the hosted checkout** with the email prefilled. The licence key is emailed and also shown on the success page with a "copy to app" deep link.
- **Purchase orders:** a "Pay by invoice / PO" option for departments (Paddle supports invoices). Universities often can't use cards.
- **Reduce friction:** no account creation; guest checkout only. Offer a 14-day refund, stated on the button.
- **Measure:** checkout start → success rate (target ≥ 60%) and drop-off by step.
