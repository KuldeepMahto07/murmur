# Global expansion

**Status: Not started (plan written).** Part of [16 · Scaling](../README.md) in the [Murmur SaaS plan](../../README.md).

- **Tax and payments:** a merchant of record (Lemon Squeezy or Paddle) covers VAT/GST and local payment methods (PayPal, iDEAL, UPI support varies; check per provider).
- **Purchasing power parity:** automatic regional pricing (for example, India, Brazil and Southeast Asia at about 40–60% of the US price) to grow the student base.
- **Localised landing pages** for the top non-English Whisper markets: Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Hindi, Japanese. Use local research-ethics wording.
- **Data-protection messaging per region:** GDPR (EU), DPDP Act (India), LGPD (Brazil). The message is the same everywhere: no data leaves the device.
