# Subscriptions

**Status: Not started (plan written).** Part of [12 · Revenue](../README.md) in the [Murmur SaaS plan](../../README.md).

Murmur leads with a **one-time licence** (that's the differentiator against cloud subscriptions). Subscriptions only appear where there's an ongoing cost or value:
- **Team server:** annual subscription (updates + support).
- **Optional "Murmur Updates" plan:** $19 a year to keep getting new versions after the first year, instead of buying an upgrade.
- **Never** subscription-gate the user's own transcripts or exports. They stay usable forever.
