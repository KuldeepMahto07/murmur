# Annual plans

- **Team server:** annual by default; a 2-year prepay at 15% off.
- **Updates plan:** annual only ($19).
- **Institutions:** align renewals to the academic year (September) and send renewal invoices 60 days ahead.
- Track annual recurring revenue (ARR) from these separately from one-time licence revenue in [13-analytics/kpi-dashboard](../../13-analytics/kpi-dashboard/).
