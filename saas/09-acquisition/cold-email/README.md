# Cold email

**Target:** research-methods instructors, graduate school coordinators, university research IT, and newsroom editors.

**Template (≤ 90 words):**
> Subject: offline transcription for interview studies
>
> Hi Dr. <Name>, I noticed your <methods course / lab> runs interview-based projects. Many ethics approvals don't allow uploading recordings to cloud transcribers. I built Murmur, which transcribes with speaker labels entirely on a Windows PC (Whisper-based). Would a free 5-seat pilot for your students be useful this term? 2-minute demo: <link>. <Your name>

**Rules:** send to work addresses of people you've researched, 20 a day max, one follow-up after 5 days, honour opt-outs immediately, and comply with CAN-SPAM/GDPR (legitimate interest, clear identity, unsubscribe line).
