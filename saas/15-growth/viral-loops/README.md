# Viral loops

**Status: Not started (plan written).** Part of [15 · Growth](../README.md) in the [Murmur SaaS plan](../../README.md).

Because transcripts are private, the loops must be **opt-in and content-free**:
- **Export footer** (off by default; on for the free tier with an easy toggle): "Transcribed locally with Murmur" in DOCX/PDF footers. Supervisors and editors see it.
- **Subtitle credit:** an optional last-cue credit in SRT/VTT for YouTubers.
- **Shareable benchmark card:** "My GPU transcribes 1 h in 4 min with Murmur", an image with no content, for social posts.
- **Never:** public transcript links or auto-sharing.
